// how it works

One story.
Four superpowers.

Four powers stacked in one deck. Tap the stack, swipe it, or use the arrows — every card flips in with its full detail and its own live effects.

01 · Write

Write it, scene by scene

Your story is chapters → scenes. Every scene is a title, prose, an optional picture and a story date. Scene 2.3 means chapter 2, scene 3 — that order is what everything else uses. The small When row under the prose sets where the scene sits in story time.

  • Drag-and-drop scene reordering
  • Artwork per scene: upload, drop or draw freehand
  • 10-step undo · autosave session recovery
  • Whole-story A4 PDF export
02 · See

See the whole story at once

The Board is a big canvas with one card per scene, connected by glowing wires. Wires leave a scene's − negative ports and enter another's ＋ positive ports — every wire gets its own dot, so nothing ever piles up.

  • Direction chevrons show how the story flows
  • Thread-bend slider: 0% razor-straight → 100% springy
  • Beautify untangles crossing wires instantly
  • Layout is saved inside your story file
03 · When

Know exactly when it happens

The Timeline runs on a calendar you build — name your years, pick how many months and days they have, invent fantasy dates. Give every scene a Year · Month · Day · Time and it drops into story time.

  • Every wire labels the time gap: +2 mo 2 d 14 h
  • ⏳ Chapters in time panel shows each chapter's span
  • Undated scenes wait in the Not dated yet strip
  • Earlier-than-neighbours scenes become dashed ⟲ flashback wires
04 · Power tools

Power tools under two keys

Press W for wire tools, S over a scene for scene tools — on the Board and the Timeline. Every item is numbered, so 1–9 fires it instantly. Keys are yours to rebind in Settings, and they never fire while you're typing.

  • ✂ Cut-knife: drag a line across wires to sever them
  • ⤳ Insert a scene mid-wire (A → C becomes A → B → C)
  • ⛓ Auto-sequence the whole story or one chapter
  • ✨ Beautify · ▦ Arrange by flow · 🗑 Remove all

// release history

Two versions. One giant leap.

DKN 1.0.0 laid the foundation. DKN 1.1.0 rebuilt how the story moves — wires, dates, power tools and a help desk.

Previous

v1.0.0

2026 · The foundation
  • Story → chapters → scenes structure
  • Add, rename, delete & drag-reorder scenes
  • Prose editor with artwork per scene
  • Freehand drawing pad: pen, colours, eraser
  • 10-step undo (button + Ctrl+Z)
  • Save / open .dknproj + autosave recovery
  • Whole-story A4 PDF export
  • Visual 2D Board + story Timeline
  • Warm-charcoal dark theme
  • Windows installer
→
Latest

v1.1.0

Oct 2026 · The leap
  • W / S tool menus — numbered wire & scene actions
  • Adjustable shortcuts — rebind W and S, safe while typing
  • Timeline rebuilt — Year · Month · Day · Time per scene
  • Time gaps everywhere — +2 mo 2 d 14 h on every wire
  • Flashbacks — dashed orange ⟲ back wires
  • ＋/− ports — pixel-exact wires with direction chevrons
  • Thread bend — straight ↔ curvy wires slider
  • Story-graph Board — auto-placed cards, layout persists
  • Help desk ❔ — searchable plain-English guide
  • News tab 📣 — what's new per version
  • Smarter translate — keeps names & loanwords in 15+ languages
